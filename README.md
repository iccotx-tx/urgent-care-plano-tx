# Urgent Care of Plano, TX

Source for the [Urgent Care of Plano information page](https://iccotx-tx.github.io/urgent-care-plano-tx/), published by Immediate Care Centers of Texas ([iccotx.com](https://iccotx.com/)).

**Urgent Care of Plano**, 3909 W Parker Rd, Unit 104, Plano, TX 75023, (469) 609-3062. Open Monday to Friday 9 AM to 7 PM and Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM, closed Sunday. This clinic treats non emergency illness and injury and is not an emergency room. **For a life threatening emergency, call 911.**

For emergencies at any hour, the group also runs Frisco ER (12600 Rolater Rd #120, Frisco, TX 75035, (469) 200-5222, open 24/7) and Castle Hills ER (4228 N Josey Ln, Carrollton, TX 75010, (469) 663-3200, open 24/7).
