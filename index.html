Urgent Care of Plano

Walk-In Care for Minor Illness & Injury in Plano, TX

Walk-In Care, No Appointment Needed

Urgent Care of Plano is a walk-in clinic operated by Immediate Care Centers of Texas at 3909 W Parker Rd #104, Plano, TX 75023. The clinic treats minor illnesses and injuries for patients of all ages, with on-site diagnostic services and a team of board-certified physicians.

Hours

Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 7 PM. Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Closed Sundays.

Part of a Larger Network

Urgent Care of Plano is one of three Immediate Care Centers of Texas locations in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. For emergencies, the network also operates two 24/7 freestanding emergency rooms: Frisco ER and Castle Hills ER in Carrollton.
